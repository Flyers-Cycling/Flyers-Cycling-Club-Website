Use this on every ride. It's short on purpose.

## Before the ride

- Check Slack and the forecast. If you need to call off the ride for lightning or mud, post it in Slack at least 2 hours before the start.
- Read the emergency action plan for today's location. Know the nearest hospital, the risky sections, and where to shelter from lightning.
- Pack a first aid kit, a phone, a pump, and basic tools.
- Know your group: who's riding, any medical notes, and a parent contact for each rider.
- Make sure there are at least two adults with the group. No adult is ever alone one-on-one with a rider.

## At the trailhead

- Take attendance.
- Check every helmet and do a quick bike check (air, brakes, chain).
- Tell the group the route, where you'll regroup, and the plan if someone gets separated.
- Name the leader and the sweep. The sweep rides last and nobody rides behind them.

## On the trail

- Regroup at every junction and at the top and bottom of hard sections.
- Ride at the pace of the slowest rider. It's their ride too.
- Watch for riders who are tired, cold, hungry, or quiet.
- If lightning moves in, head for the shelter spot in the plan.

## After the ride

- Take attendance again before anyone leaves.
- Stay until every rider has been picked up. Two adults stay until the last rider leaves.
- Tell parents about any crash or injury, even a small one.
- Post anything the other coaches should know in Slack.
