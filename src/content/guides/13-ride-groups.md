Good groups make for good rides. These ideas come from NICA's coach education.

## Ways to group riders

- **By ability and fitness.** Rides go at a pace that fits the group. Watch that nobody feels left behind.
- **Mixed ages and abilities.** Older riders mentor younger ones, and it builds team spirit. This works best on skills days, since trail rides are hard to keep together.
- **By goals.** Ask riders what they want from the season. In Flyers XC, that's one reason we have Flyers XC Devo and Flyers XC Comp.

## Setting groups early in the season

- Run a skills day and note what each rider can do.
- Ask the coaches who've ridden with each kid.
- Let riders choose between an easier and a harder group, and tell them up front that groups may change.

## During the season

Keep groups steady so riders and coaches get to know each other. Be ready to combine or split groups when coaches are short or the practice plan changes.

Talk with the other coaches after each practice about how the groups went.
