Each skill builds on the one before it. Teach on easy ground first, then take it to the trail.

1. **Ready position.** Level pedals, standing, knees and elbows bent, eyes up. Everything else starts here.
2. **Braking.** Both brakes, smooth and steady. Brake before a turn, not in it.
3. **Shifting.** Shift before a climb, while pedaling lightly.
4. **Looking ahead.** Look where you want to go, not at the rock you want to miss.
5. **Cornering.** Brake early, look through the turn, and lean the bike more than the body.
6. **Climbing.** Steady pedal stroke, weight forward on steep pitches, pick a smooth line.
7. **Small obstacles.** Roots, rocks, and ledges, with the ready position and a light front wheel.
8. **Descending.** Weight back and low, brakes under control, let the bike move under you.
9. **Drops and jumps.** Only once the earlier skills are solid. Start small.

Move a rider on when they can do a skill well and calmly, not just once. Flyers Enduro/Gravity riders spend more time on 7 through 9.
