## Learn to ride downhill with control

Utah has great descending trails, and Flyers Enduro/Gravity, the largest youth enduro program in the country, helps young riders learn to ride them safely. Volunteer coaches teach one step at a time, so riders build skills before they take on harder terrain.

### What riders work on

In enduro racing, riders pedal up untimed climbs and race timed downhill stages. Riders need to handle their bikes and look after themselves on the trail, so coaches focus on steady progress over "just sending it":

- **The basics:** braking zones, letting the bike move under you, choosing a line, and controlled drops and jumps.
- **Race skills (optional):** walking a course, remembering blind sections, pacing over several stages, and fixing common mechanicals on the trail.
- **Team days at races:** a shared shade tent, bike checks, and teammates cheering each other on.

### Where we ride

Most rides are close to home on Eagle Mountain and Lake Mountain. We also head out to Kyhv Peak (formerly Squaw Peak), Sundance, Deer Valley, Solitude, and other spots.

### Races, if you want them

Riders who want to race follow the youth enduro series. Downhill riders race the Mountain States Cup and the Monster Pro Series. Racing is never required.
