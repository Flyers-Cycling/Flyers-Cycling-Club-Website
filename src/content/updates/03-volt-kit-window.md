Our **Volt Cyclewear** kit store opens Dec 1, the same day as registration, and closes Dec 15. Orders arrive before spring rides and races. Team kits are optional. The jerseys and Alpine Thermal layers are made to go with plain black bibs or mountain bike shorts you may already own, which keeps the cost down.
