Registration for the 2027 Flyers XC and Flyers Enduro/Gravity season opens **Tuesday, Dec 1**. Jr. Flyers registration opens later, and we'll share the date when it's set. We'll post the sign-up link on the Registration page of this site that day.

Riders can join more than one program, like Flyers Enduro/Gravity plus Flyers XC. Riders need to be 10 or older for Flyers Enduro/Gravity.

The season starts with our winter kickoff ride on Sat, Jan 2.
