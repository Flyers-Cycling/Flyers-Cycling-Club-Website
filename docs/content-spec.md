## Site-wide

- **Header:** program quick links, main nav (Programs, Schedule, New Families, Coaches, Board, Sponsors, About, FAQ; Gear & News is in the mobile menu and footer), and the Register and Sponsor or donate buttons.
- **Footer:** legal entity, programs, club links (including Registration, New Families, Coaches, and Board), and support links.
- **Mission:** `siteConfig.mission` in `src/data/site.ts`. It's shown on the home page and the About page.
